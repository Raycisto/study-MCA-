Unit 1 · Number System
Factors · LCM · HCF · Remainders · Unit Digit 0 / 20 done
▼
🔑 Key Formulas & Shortcuts Number of factors of N = aˣ × bʸ × cᶻ ... → (x+1)(y+1)(z+1)
HCF × LCM = Product of two numbers
Remainder Rule: (a×b) mod n = [(a mod n) × (b mod n)] mod n
Unit digit of aⁿ cycles: 2→{2,4,8,6}, 3→{3,9,7,1}, 7→{7,9,3,1}, 8→{8,4,2,6}
Unit digit of powers of 0,1,5,6 → always 0,1,5,6
Divisibility by 9: sum of digits divisible by 9

💡 Quick Tips

• To find HCF of big numbers: use Euclid's Division — HCF(a,b) = HCF(b, a mod b)
• LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators
• HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators
• Remainder of (10ⁿ - 1) ÷ 9 = 0 always

"How many ways to arrange?" → P  |  "How many ways to choose?" → C
